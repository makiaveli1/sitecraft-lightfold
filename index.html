01 / Form

One silhouette.
Seven decisions.

The body is not a shell. Seven narrow planes lean toward one another, each holding enough distance to let light escape at the edges.

No plane repeats the exact angle of the one before it.

02 / Light

The glow lives
between surfaces.

A warm core is hidden inside the fold. What reaches the room is reflected light, softened by metal before it reaches the eye.

03 / Mechanism

Nothing decorative
is holding it together.

The same narrow spine that carries power also sets the spacing of the seven planes. The object can open for service without changing its exterior language.

  1. 01 folded aluminium plane
  2. 02 concealed hinge rail
  3. 03 reflected light channel
  4. 04 weighted mineral base

04 / Scale

Closer to furniture
than a lamp.

At 1.26 metres, LIGHTFOLD sits below eye level but above the furniture line. It is meant to alter the edge of a room, not dominate its centre.

01260 mm

05 / Finish

Let the material
change the temperature.

The form stays fixed. Finish changes how much of the light is absorbed, reflected, or allowed to disappear into the room.
